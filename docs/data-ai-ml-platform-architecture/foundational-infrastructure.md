# Foundational Infrastructure

These are the shared platform primitives behind modern data, AI, and ML systems.

---

## 1. Data Ingestion & Connectors
Brings in product, event, model, and third-party data from many sources.

## 2. Storage Layer / Lakehouse / Warehouse Foundations
Stores raw, transformed, and derived data across multiple access patterns.

## 3. Metadata Catalog & Lineage
Tracks datasets, schemas, ownership, and dependency relationships.

## 4. Orchestration & Scheduling
Coordinates batch and streaming pipelines, retraining, and evaluation workflows.

## 5. Compute Execution Layer
Runs transformations, training jobs, notebooks, and inference backends.

## 6. Feature, Embedding or Artifact Storage
Stores reusable derived assets for models and product features.

## 7. Model Registry & Version Management
Tracks model versions, artifacts, release state, and provenance.

## 8. Inference Serving Infrastructure
Serves models or retrieval stacks behind APIs with latency and cost control.

## 9. Evaluation, Experimentation & Benchmarking
Measures model quality, regression risk, and product impact.

## 10. Observability, Monitoring & Cost Tracking
Tracks data quality, training health, inference performance, and spend.

## 11. Security, Governance & Access Control
Controls access to data, models, prompts, and operational capabilities.

## 12. Human Feedback, Labeling & Review Workflows
Supports annotation, curation, and human-in-the-loop quality loops.

## 13. APIs, SDKs & Product Integration Surface
Makes platform capabilities consumable by applications and developers.

## 14. Billing, Quotas & Usage Policies
Enforces resource and product limits across tenants and workloads.

## 15. Release, Rollout & Safety Controls
Supports safe deployment of models, prompts, and retrieval changes.

## 16. Support, Debugging & Reproducibility Tooling
Makes it possible to inspect data, models, experiments, and incidents after the fact.
